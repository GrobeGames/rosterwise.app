Common first questions

The things new families ask us

What is RosterWise, exactly?
A college-recruiting intelligence app paired with a free guide library. The app helps families find the programs where their athlete fits — using real, public roster data on every program — and the guides explain the whole process along the way.
How is it different from a recruiting-profile service?
Those services help your athlete market themselves to coaches. RosterWise helps you decide where to aim — the data, roster analysis, and fit scores on every program. It’s a different job, and there’s really nothing else like it for families.
How much does it cost?
$39.99 one time. No subscriptions, no monthly fees, no ads — less than a single tournament entry fee, with access for your entire recruiting journey.
Is there a free version?
Yes — the entire guide library and every sport section on this site are free, with no signup. The app is the paid tool ($39.99 once) that adds the per-program data and fit scores.
Do I need to create an account?
No. There’s no login or registration. Your athlete’s profile stays on your device — your recruiting journey is your business.
What sports do you cover?
Apps for Soccer, Volleyball, Lacrosse, and Wrestling are available now, with Softball on the way. This site has full recruiting sections for all four current sports.
