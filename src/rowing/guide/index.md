These guides cover college rowing for both women's and men's teams, with the differences called out inside each guide. For the overviews of each side of the sport, see [women's college rowing](/rowing/womens/) and [men's college rowing](/rowing/mens/).

Each guide is built from primary sources, lists them at the bottom, and is dated to when we read them. Where we couldn't confirm a rule from the body that sets it, we leave it out and suggest the question to ask instead.

For topics that apply across sports, such as the NCAA Eligibility Center and the House settlement, see our universal [recruiting guides](/guide/).
