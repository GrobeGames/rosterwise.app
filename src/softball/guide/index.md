These guides cover college softball's own rules, read from the governing bodies' documents: the NCAA Division I, II and III Manuals, the NCAA's recruiting calendars, the NAIA Handbook and the NJCAA Bylaws. Each page lists its sources and the date we read them. For how many programs there are at each level, start at the [softball hub](/softball/).

Rules that apply in every sport, such as the House settlement, the NCAA Eligibility Center, official and unofficial visits, and the transfer portal, are in our [universal recruiting guides](/guide/).
