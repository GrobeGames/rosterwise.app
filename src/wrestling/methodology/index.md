RosterWise doesn't guess, estimate, or rely on reputation. Every insight comes from systematic analysis of published college wrestling rosters — read the way wrestling is actually organized: **by weight class**. This section explains exactly how that analysis works.

## Why methodology matters

Most recruiting services hand you a list of schools and tell you to "reach out." RosterWise takes a different approach: we analyze the data programs publish — rosters, weight-class lineups, schedules, coaching staff — and surface the patterns that inform smarter decisions. Families deserve to understand how we reach our conclusions, including what the data can and cannot tell you.

## The five dimensions

RosterWise analyzes every college wrestling program across five dimensions:

1. **[Roster composition by weight class](/wrestling/methodology/roster-composition/)** — the foundation of everything else
2. **[Weight-class depth & class-year gaps](/wrestling/methodology/weight-class-depth/)** — who wrestles your weight, and when they graduate
3. **[Coach tenure](/wrestling/methodology/coach-tenure/)** — stability and program-direction signals
4. **[Recruiting geography](/wrestling/methodology/recruiting-geography/)** — the pipelines a program draws from
5. **[RosterFit scoring](/wrestling/methodology/rosterfit-score/)** — personalized fit based on your athlete's weight, class year, level, and location

Together, these produce a complete picture of every program's recruiting landscape — not from rankings or reputation, but from what the roster data actually shows. Where "level" is involved, our analysis carries the women's unified-championship caveat so it means the right thing for each gender.
