## 1. NCAA men's and women's playing-rules changes for the 2027 season (shot clock, draw, video review, etc.)

### Takeaway
Both sports adopted new 2027-season rules in July and August 2026, so this is new since the fact log's 2026-08-26 pass.
- **Men's:** fluorescent green/yellow ball (allowed now, required later), 15-minute overtime periods, and a wider coach's challenge and head/neck video review. The men's shot clock is unchanged.
- **Women's:** 13 changes, including:
  - every yellow card becomes a two-minute non-releasable penalty
  - a new "targeting" red card
  - red cards no longer carry an automatic next-game suspension
  - flopping and embellishment merged into one foul
  - the 2026 "keep possession at the quarter break" rule rolled back to the 2025 power-play rule
  - only coaches may request video review

Both sports are also running fall-only **experimental** rules, including women's 10-a-side play and a 60-second possession reset after a draw. These were not adopted.

### Cited Findings

**Men's, 2027 season**
- The **DI Men's Lacrosse Oversight Committee approved five rules changes from the DI Men's Lacrosse Rules Subcommittee** at its July 27-28, 2026 annual meeting:
  - **(a)** The standard ball color becomes "fluorescent green or yellow, similar to hex code #ccff00. This is allowable for the 2027 season and will be fully required by the 2028 season."
  - **(b)** "Changing overtime play to proceed for 15-minute periods or until a goal is scored. Timeout and challenge rules remain unchanged."
  - **(c)** A coach's challenge may now target a goal that could be negated by offsides, too many players on the field, or a ball carrier stepping on a boundary line. Boundary lines include the midline and restraining lines. The challenge is allowed "only if the goal was scored within 15 seconds of when the violation occurred and no whistle has sounded" in between. Officials may use a stopwatch if the clock is not burned into the video.
  - **(d)** Officials may start video review of possible head/neck contact without having made a call.
  - **(e)** Officials may rescind a head/neck penalty if video shows no illegal contact.
  - Source: *Report of the NCAA Division I Men's Lacrosse Oversight Committee, July 27-28, 2026, Annual Meeting*. NCAA. Report dated July 27-28, 2026. https://ncaaorg.s3.amazonaws.com/championships/sports/lacrosse/d1/men/JUL2026D1MLA_Report.pdf. T1. Read 2026-10-08.
- The **NCAA.org news release confirms all three divisions.** The DI oversight committee approved "Monday" and the **Divisions II and III Playing Rules Oversight Panel (PROP)** approved "the week before."
  - The release describes ball color as a recommendation for 2026-27 and a requirement starting in 2027-28. This matches the report's "allowable 2027 season / required 2028 season."
  - It also says overtime moves from **four-minute periods with teams switching sides to 15-minute periods** until a goal is scored.
  - It announces an **optional fall-only experimental rule** for all three divisions: a ball released below the defensive-end restraining line counts as a shot only if it scores or hits the goal piping. The rule targets teams throwing the ball from their defensive end to exploit a time violation.
  - The release gives **no** effective season for the overtime change and does **not** mention the shot clock or faceoffs.
  - Source: "Expanded video review approved in men's lacrosse." NCAA.org, Greg Johnson. July 28, 2026. https://www.ncaa.org/expanded-video-review-approved-in-mens-lacrosse/. T1. Read 2026-10-08.
- **The NCAA had already chosen the ball and net colors for its own championship.** The DI Men's Lacrosse Oversight Committee "adopted optic yellow as the game ball color and black as the game net color for the 2027-28 Division I Men's Lacrosse Championship … already allowable in the playing rules."
  - Source: *Report of the NCAA DI Men's Lacrosse Oversight Committee, March 2, 2026*. NCAA. https://ncaaorg.s3.amazonaws.com/championships/sports/lacrosse/d1/men/MAR2026D1MLA_Report.pdf. T1. Read 2026-10-08. Older item; its effect lands in 2027-28.

**Women's, 2027 season**
- The **DI Women's Lacrosse Rules Subcommittee and the DII/III Women's Lacrosse Rules Committee** met jointly June 15-18, 2026. They approved **13 rules-change proposals "for the 2027 season,"** each marked "Effective date. Immediately":
  - **(a)** Equipment that needs certification or governing-body approval must be commercially available to all institutions **by January 1 preceding the spring season**.
  - **(b)** Rule 2-2 stringing: strung pockets attach only through the stringing holes. Mesh is allowed if the gap is no more than two nylons (0.6 cm).
  - **(c)** Rule 2-6: at least **one stick check of both drawers' sticks every quarter**, plus before each overtime opening draw.
  - **(d)** Rule 4-1: the game and possession clocks stop during official warnings and during defensive false starts on 8-meter free positions.
  - **(e)** Rule 5-1: the 2025 power-play rule is **reinstated** in modified form. A team with a numerical advantage that keeps possession at the end of a quarter or overtime period keeps it to start the next. "The 2026 rule permitting any team in possession at the end of the first and third quarters to retain possession … regardless of any numerical advantage, is no longer applicable."
  - **(f)** Rules 5-51 to 5-61: **only coaches may request video review.** Officials must notify both coaches and should "strive to complete the review within two minutes." The report calls 2026 video review's "first season as a permissive rule."
  - **(g)** Embellishment and flopping are combined into one **flagrant** foul, carrying a yellow card and a two-minute non-releasable penalty.
  - **(h)** Rule 6-6: players may **exchange sticks** on the field when not in possession. This partly reverses the 2026 ban on switching sticks on the field.
  - **(i)** Egregious-conduct and misconduct rules are revised.
  - **(j)** **Targeting** is added as a new flagrant foul, and it is a **red card**.
  - **(k)** **All yellow cards** become two-minute non-releasable penalties.
  - **(l)** The red card no longer carries an **automatic next-game suspension**, though the in-game ejection stays.
  - **(m)** The penalty structure is revised again.
  - The joint rules report is attached to the DI oversight committee report, which records that the committee "reviewed and adopted 13 playing rules proposals developed by the Division I Women's Lacrosse Rules Subcommittee."
  - Source: *Report of the NCAA DI Women's Lacrosse Oversight Committee, July 28-29, 2026, Annual Meeting*, including the attached *Report of the DI Women's Lacrosse Rules Subcommittee and DII/III Women's Lacrosse Rules Committee, June 15-18, 2026*. NCAA. https://ncaaorg.s3.amazonaws.com/championships/sports/lacrosse/d1/women/Jul2026D1WLA_Report.pdf. T1. Read 2026-10-08.
- The **NCAA.org release** confirms the DI oversight committee approved these "last week" and PROP approved them "Tuesday," effective "for the 2026-27 academic year."
  - Video review will "expand": it now covers any yellow or red card to identify the offender, and flopping is reviewable. After a goal, a request must come before the draw clock expires. During a timeout, it must come in the first 30 seconds.
  - **Fall experimental rules**, not adopted:
    - the possession clock resets to **60 instead of 90 seconds** after a draw
    - **10 instead of 12 players** on the field
    - **six instead of seven** players required below the restraining line
    - the defense gets possession if the attack fails to keep the ball below the restraining line
  - Source: "Simplified penalty structure approved in women's lacrosse." NCAA.org, Greg Johnson. August 12, 2026. https://www.ncaa.org/simplified-penalty-structure-approved-in-womens-lacrosse/. T1. Read 2026-10-08.

**Women's, already in force for 2026 (published 2025, still current for 2026-27)**
- The **2026 and 2027 NCAA Women's Lacrosse Rules** book, dated February 2026, sets these baselines:
  - "four 15-minute quarters"
  - a **90-second possession clock**
  - "Twelve players"
  - field players **may choose** headgear meeting the current ASTM standard (see §4)
  - draws "at the start of the game, at halftime," at each overtime, and after goals
  - Source: *2026 and 2027 NCAA Women's Lacrosse Rules*. NCAA, February 2026. https://ncaaorg.s3.amazonaws.com/championships/sports/lacrosse/rules/women/PRWLA_RulesBook.pdf. T1. Read 2026-10-08.
- The **2026-season change list** (approved 2025) includes:
  - a 30-second draw-setup clock
  - possession retention at the end of quarters 1 and 3, which the 2027 rules now reverse
  - two-minute rest plus a **five-minute sudden-victory overtime**
  - the first penalty restructure
  - video review with **two team requests per game**
  - Source: *2026 and 2027 Women's Lacrosse Rules Changes*. NCAA (PDF metadata dated August 14, 2025). https://ncaaorg.s3.amazonaws.com/championships/sports/lacrosse/rules/women/2025-26PRWLA_RulesChanges.pdf. T1. Read 2026-10-08. Older item.

## 2. Championship changes: bracket size, automatic qualifiers, sites, format (DI, DII, DIII)

### Takeaway
No bracket-size change takes effect for 2027 in DI men's, DI women's or DII men's.
- The **DI men's** committee wants to expand but says sponsorship does not yet support it.
- The **DI women's** committee wants **one more team in 2028** (and ideally 32), but that is not adopted.

The big structural news is that **DI, DII and DIII women's lacrosse will hold a joint finals weekend for the first time**, at Towson in 2027 and Subaru Park in Philadelphia in 2028. This mirrors the men's joint championship, which returns to **Philadelphia (Lincoln Financial Field, host Drexel) in 2027**. DII adopted the **NCAA Power Index (NPI) as its sole at-large selection criterion** starting in 2026-27.

### Cited Findings

**DI men's**
- **2027 automatic qualifiers:** "all 10 eligible conferences receive one automatic qualification spot." They are America East, ASUN, Atlantic 10, Big East, Big Ten, CAA, Ivy, MAAC, NEC and Patriot.
- **Bracket:** members noted "despite their wanting to expand the bracket, sponsorship is not yet such to support proposing to do so."
- **Selection criteria:** RPI language was clarified, and "trend of performance and competitive in losses" were added as secondary criteria. A coaches-proposed metric ("Powerwise") is under discussion and not adopted.
- **Sites:** "Brown University and the U.S. Naval Academy will be hosting the quarterfinals May 22-23, 2027. Drexel University will host the men's championships May 29-31, 2027."
- Source: *DI Men's Lacrosse Oversight Committee report, July 27-28, 2026*. NCAA. https://ncaaorg.s3.amazonaws.com/championships/sports/lacrosse/d1/men/JUL2026D1MLA_Report.pdf. T1. Read 2026-10-08.

## 3. Recruiting calendar 2026-27, House roster limits, program adds and drops, conference realignment

### Takeaway
The site's 2026-27 calendar and contact-date content still stands. The D1 Manual stamped 10/8/26 matches the fact log's calendar rows and confirms both "normal hours" start times. Roster limits are unchanged at **48 (men) / 38 (women)**.

## 4. Women's lacrosse equipment and headgear (NCAA, USA Lacrosse)

### Takeaway
There is **no headgear mandate** for NCAA women's field players. Headgear stays optional and must meet the current ASTM women's lacrosse standard if worn. Eye protection is required.

The equipment changes are:
- for **2027**, gear needing certification must be commercially available by **January 1** before the season
- new stringing and pocket rules
- **officials** (not players) must use headsets in **DI and DII starting in 2027**; DIII is exempt

### Cited Findings
- **Headgear and eyewear, 2026 and 2027 Rules book:**
  - **Headgear:** "Any field player choosing to wear headgear will only be permitted to wear headgear that meets the current ASTM standard for women's lacrosse and is labeled accordingly. All ASTM women's lacrosse headgear that is approved for play will be listed on the USA Lacrosse website." (Rule 2, Section 20)
  - **Eyewear:** "All field players shall wear eye protection properly." (Rule 2, Section 18)
  - Source: *2026 and 2027 NCAA Women's Lacrosse Rules*. NCAA, February 2026. https://ncaaorg.s3.amazonaws.com/championships/sports/lacrosse/rules/women/PRWLA_RulesBook.pdf. T1. Read 2026-10-08.
- **Headsets, same rules book, Rule 3-10.d:** "The use of headsets is strongly encouraged for the 2026 season and shall be required beginning with the 2027 season, **except in Division III**, where headsets are recommended but not required for the 2026 and 2027 seasons." Same source. T1.
  - This **conflicts with** the August 2025 *2026 and 2027 Women's Lacrosse Rules Changes* PDF, which says 2027 headsets are required for "all officials." **The later February 2026 rules book governs.** https://ncaaorg.s3.amazonaws.com/championships/sports/lacrosse/rules/women/2025-26PRWLA_RulesChanges.pdf. T1.
- **New 2027 equipment rules:**
  - certification-required gear must be commercially available by January 1
  - stringing: mesh gap no more than 0.6 cm
  - drawers' sticks are checked every quarter
  - Source: July 28-29, 2026 DI WLA report, Attachment A. T1 (§1).
