## 1. Women's volleyball: championship bracket and site, playing season and first-contest dates, 2026 and 2027 rules changes

### Takeaway
The 2026 D1 championship keeps its 64-team single-elimination format, with the final four at the Alamodome in San Antonio on Dec. 17 and 20, 2026. The NCAA's own figures conflict on the automatic-qualifier count (31 vs 32). The playing-rules changes for 2026 and 2027 are real and Tier 1 documented. The most consequential pending item is a D1 proposal for one fixed first-contest date (effective Aug. 1, 2027, if adopted), alongside an AVCA-backed playing and practice season model that is still under discussion. Neither has been decided.

### Cited Findings

**Championship format, site and dates (2026)**
- The 2026 D1 women's volleyball championship field is 64 teams, single elimination. "Of the 64 teams, 31 teams receive automatic qualification while the remaining 33 teams will be selected on an at-large basis." Selection show: Sunday, Nov. 29, 2026. First and second rounds: Dec. 3–5, 2026, at non-predetermined campus sites. Regionals: Dec. 10–13, 2026, at campus sites. National semifinals: Dec. 17, 2026; final: Dec. 20, 2026, at the Alamodome, San Antonio. — [NCAA.com, "Women's college volleyball championship: Road to the championship" (page undated; 2026 content); Tier 1; read 2026-10-08](https://www.ncaa.com/championships/volleyball-women/d1/road-to-the-championship)
- **CONFLICT (Tier 1 vs Tier 1) on the number of automatic qualifiers:** the D1 Women's Volleyball Oversight Committee "discussed automatic qualification submissions and approved the 32 AQs as provided in the materials." — [NCAA, "Report of the NCAA Division I Women's Volleyball Oversight Committee, Feb. 16–18, 2026, Videoconference," Informational Item 3; Tier 1; read 2026-10-08](https://ncaaorg.s3.amazonaws.com/championships/sports/volleyball/d1/women/WVBOC/FEB2026D1WVBOC_Feb16Report.pdf). This contradicts NCAA.com's "31 teams receive automatic qualification." Unresolved. Do not publish an AQ count without settling it.
- NCAA.org confirms "December 17 & 20, 2026," Alamodome, San Antonio, TX. Its posted manuals are still the 2025-26 Kansas City set. — [NCAA.org, "Division I Women's Volleyball" championship page; Tier 1; read 2026-10-08](https://www.ncaa.org/championship/division-i/womens-volleyball/)
- Bid-spec date formula: semifinals and finals on the "Thursday and Saturday after regionals," with the traditional window "December 17-19 or 20" for 2026-27 and "December 16-18 or 19" for 2027-28. — [NCAA, "2026 and 2027 NCAA Division I Volleyball … Sport Specific Information" (bid specifications, 2026-28 cycle; undated); Tier 1; read 2026-10-08](https://ncaaorg.s3.amazonaws.com/championships/marketing/bids/2026-28BidSpec/2026-28D1WVB_BID_SPECS.pdf). **Playing season and first-contest date (D1)**
- **[OLDER, applies to 2026-27]** Blanket waiver approved Jan. 29, 2026: D1 programs may "begin preseason practice sessions in women's volleyball on July 30, 2026." The playing season stays limited to 132 days. Approved Feb. 18, 2026 for the 2026-27 year: "up to two scrimmages or exhibition contests prior to the first scheduled regular-season contest," which must fall within the declared playing season and count against the maximum number of contests. Both entries say the oversight committee "intends to propose legislation to amend the women's volleyball playing and practice season … prior to fall 2027." — [NCAA.org, "Division I Legislative Relief Blanket Waivers" (Case Nos. 1261912 and 1263191); Tier 1; read 2026-10-08](https://www.ncaa.org/division-i/governance/legislation-and-policy/legislative-relief-blanket-waivers/)
- **[OLDER, May 21, 2026; would take effect 2027-28]** The D1 WVB Oversight Committee recommended that the D1 Cabinet "introduce a proposal into the 2026-27 legislative cycle to specify that an institution shall not … play its first contest with outside competition until the Thursday preceding the Saturday that is 15 weeks before the first Saturday in December, and to eliminate the first contest date exception for the AVCA Showcase." Effective date: Aug. 1, 2027. Rationale given: more televised matches and fewer conflicts with football scheduling. It "does not increase the 132-day playing season." — [NCAA, "Report of the NCAA DI Women's Volleyball Oversight Committee, May 21, 2026, Videoconference"; Tier 1; read 2026-10-08](https://ncaaorg.s3.amazonaws.com/championships/sports/volleyball/d1/women/WVBOC/MAY2026D1WVBOC_May21Report.pdf)
- Sept. 3, 2026: the committee "discussed amending the first permissible practice date to 22 days prior to the first scheduled regular-season intercollegiate contest in combination with an additional four-week practice period to occur between the end of the spring term or quarter and the start of the preseason practice period." Discussion only, no action. — [NCAA, "Report of the NCAA DI Women's Volleyball Oversight Committee, Sept. 3, 2026, Videoconference" (document stamp 09_24_2026), Informational Item 1; Tier 1; read 2026-10-08](https://ncaaorg.s3.amazonaws.com/championships/sports/volleyball/d1/women/WVBOC/Sept2026D1WVB_Report.pdf)
- The committee repeatedly discussed "a playing and practice season model from the AVCA" (Feb., April 16 and May 7, 2026 reports). Its content is not published in those reports. — [NCAA, WVB Oversight Committee report, April 16, 2026; Tier 1; read 2026-10-08](https://ncaaorg.s3.amazonaws.com/championships/sports/volleyball/d1/women/WVBOC/APR2026D1WVBOC_Apr16Report.pdf); [May 7, 2026 report; Tier 1](https://ncaaorg.s3.amazonaws.com/championships/sports/volleyball/d1/women/WVBOC/MAY2026D1WVBOC_May7Report.pdf)
- **[OLDER, effective Aug. 1, 2026]** The oversight committee **adopted** Proposal No. 2026-29 (removes the ground-transportation-only restriction for nonchampionship-segment travel) and Proposal No. 2026-19 (removes the restriction on hosting competition involving prospects in conjunction with an intercollegiate competition), both effective Aug. 1, 2026. It **tabled** Proposal No. 2026-28. — [NCAA, WVB Oversight Committee report, May 7, 2026; Tier 1; read 2026-10-08](https://ncaaorg.s3.amazonaws.com/championships/sports/volleyball/d1/women/WVBOC/MAY2026D1WVBOC_May7Report.pdf). The Feb. 2026 report describes 2026-19 as letting schools host "a high school, preparatory or two-year college competition in conjunction with an intercollegiate competition." It also introduced a proposal letting schools play in the AVCA Showcase "no more than twice every three years" (effective immediately if adopted). — [Feb. 16–18, 2026 report; Tier 1](https://ncaaorg.s3.amazonaws.com/championships/sports/volleyball/d1/women/WVBOC/FEB2026D1WVBOC_Feb16Report.pdf)
- Oct. 7, 2026: the D1 Cabinet introduced a decentralization package for all sports under which "Playing seasons would have standardized dates for starting practices," with standard dates for starting regular-season competition and ending the regular season. It also changes official-visit rules (schools may arrange transportation, lodging and entertainment for a prospect and four accompanying individuals). The Cabinet could adopt it "as early as January." Volleyball is not mentioned. — [NCAA.org, "Division I Cabinet introduces legislation to modernize NCAA rules," Meghan Durham Wright, 2026-10-07; Tier 1; read 2026-10-08](https://www.ncaa.org/news/division-i-cabinet-introduces-legislation-to-modernize-ncaa-rules/)

**2026 and 2027 playing-rules changes (published May 12, 2026; effective for the 2026 and 2027 seasons)**
- D1 changes, "approved by the NCAA Division I Women's Volleyball Rules Subcommittee and the Division I Women's Volleyball Oversight Committee. All rules changes are effective with the 2026 and 2027 seasons" — [NCAA, "2026 and 2027 Division I Women's Volleyball Rules Changes" (PDF created 2026-05-12); Tier 1; read 2026-10-08](https://ncaaorg.s3.amazonaws.com/championships/sports/volleyball/rules/women/2026-27PRWVB_D1RulesChanges.pdf):
  - **Rule 13.2.4, Screening:** "Any player on the serving team is not permitted to raise their hands above the head during service until the ball has passed beyond the net."
  - **Rules 15.2.4.3 and 18.1.4, Center line:** "Completely crossing the center line with the foot or feet is not permitted." Crossing with other body parts is allowed if there is no interference. A center-line violation is now a reviewable (challengeable) decision.
  - **Rule 18.1.5, Challenge procedures:** the head coach requests a challenge by forming a "C" with the hands, and a challenge "must be requested immediately after the play … and before a timeout is initiated."
  - **Rule 8.1.4:** referees may consult official match statistics and/or the challenge review system to resolve a scoring discrepancy without a protest.
  - **Rule 15.1.1, Pursuit:** a ball that crosses outside the antenna into the opponent's free zone may be played back, as in men's volleyball. Not used in facilities without 2 m of clearance.
  - **Rule 11.2.3:** an alternative technical-timeout protocol (at 15 points in sets 1–4, 90 seconds maximum; two 75-second team timeouts per set; no media timeout in set 5).
  - **Rules 9.2.4.3 and 9.2.5.1:** teams change sides only after the second set and stay put in the fifth set.
  - **Rule 6.1.2:** misconduct sanctions carry over for the whole match. **Rule 6.1.2.3:** two-match suspension if a disqualified player or coach fails to sit out. **Rule 7.2.2:** jewelry above the chin allowed. **Rule 1.2.2:** up to two commercial or nonprofit logos on court.
  - **Experimental Rule 6.1.5:** conferences may experiment in 2026 with live statistics and/or live video to the bench for coaching. It may not be used to show referees a missed call.
- **No libero rule change** appears in either the D1 or the D2/D3 2026-27 rules-change document (both read in full).
- D2/D3 changes match the D1 list and add **Rule 11.3.2.1**: substitutions per set rise "from 15 to 18." — [NCAA, "2026 and 2027 Divisions II and III Women's Volleyball Rules Changes" (PDF created 2026-05-12); Tier 1; read 2026-10-08](https://ncaaorg.s3.amazonaws.com/championships/sports/volleyball/rules/women/2026-27PRWVB_D23RulesChanges.pdf)
- The D1 committee approved "12 rules changes and one experimental rule" in Feb. 2026. It also continues to work on challenge-review-system minimum standards and an NCAA RFP, and on whether to use RPI in selections ("will continue discussions … and explore additional metrics"). — [Feb. 16–18, 2026 report, Informational Items 4, 8, 10; Tier 1](https://ncaaorg.s3.amazonaws.com/championships/sports/volleyball/d1/women/WVBOC/FEB2026D1WVBOC_Feb16Report.pdf); [April 16, 2026 report, Item 3; Tier 1](https://ncaaorg.s3.amazonaws.com/championships/sports/volleyball/d1/women/WVBOC/APR2026D1WVBOC_Apr16Report.pdf)
- NAIA women's volleyball adopted "2026 and 2027 NCAA Women's Volleyball Rule Changes, excluding experimental rule 6.1.5," effective 2026-2027. — [NAIA, "2026-27 Women's Volleyball Policy Updates" (last modified 8/14/26); Tier 1; read 2026-10-08](https://www.naia.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/WVB-NAC-Update-1.pdf)

## 6. Volleyball-specific effects of the age-based eligibility rule

### Takeaway
The NCAA's adoption article names no sport. Its effects on volleyball are only the general ones (five years for athletes who enroll by the academic year after their 19th birthday; sport-specific and redshirt rules eliminated; fall 2027 enrollees fully covered). No primary source found says anything specific to volleyball.

### Cited Findings
- June 23, 2026: Division I adopted an age-based eligibility model. Athletes who start college by the academic year after their 19th birthday get up to five years of eligibility. It eliminates "season-of-competition limits, sport-specific eligibility and redshirt rules, and eligibility extension waivers." Full application begins with prospects first enrolling full time in fall 2027. Fall 2026 enrollees and current athletes get whichever rules are more favorable. Waiver requests under the old rules were due by July 31, 2026. Volleyball is not mentioned. — [NCAA.org, "Division I adopts age-based eligibility model," 2026-06-23; Tier 1; read 2026-10-08](https://www.ncaa.org/news/division-i-adopts-age-based-eligibility-model/)

International men's volleyball recruits, whom the site describes as often older, would be affected by an age rule. But no primary source makes a volleyball-specific statement, so this is inference and must not be published as a sourced claim.
