## Q1. Weight classes, rules-book changes (2026-27 / 2027-28 cycle), weight-management and weigh-in rules

### Takeaway
Men's wrestling is in the second year of the 2025-26/2026-27 rules book: no new playing-rule or weight-class changes for 2026-27, and the next rules-change meeting is April 2027 (for 2027-28). The 2026-27 men's (Aug 11, 2026) and women's (Aug 18, 2026) weight-management packets both state "no changes" to descent rules and keep 1.020 / 1.5% / 5% (men) / 17% (women). The open governance item is women's rules ownership. In March 2026 the NCAA women's committee recommended an NCAA-owned women's rules book, a secretary-rules editor and a rules committee effective Jan. 1, 2027. A Sept. 17, 2026 NCAA schedule still lists the NWCA leadership group as the women's rules source. Status: **undecided / not confirmed**.

## Q2. Women's wrestling: NCAA championship status (D-I/II/III), sponsorship counts, NAIA/NJCAA women's changes

### Takeaway
The settled structure the site describes is confirmed. The site's **sponsorship numbers are stale**, though:
- The NCAA itself said in Jan 2026 that **55 D-III schools** sponsor the sport, "more than half" of all sponsors.
- Iowa State (T1, Apr 2026) says **113 NCAA members** sponsor it.
- Columbia (T1, Jun 2026) says "more than 100".
- The NCAA's July 2026 regional alignment for 2027 assigns **120 institutions** to six regions.

On the structure:
- The 2027 National Collegiate championship is **March 5–6, 2027, Xtream Arena, Coralville, Iowa**: 180 qualifiers from **six regionals (30 each)** on Feb. 19–21, 2027.

## Q3. Championship format/qualification/site changes; 2026-27 recruiting calendar; House roster limits; transfer windows

### Takeaway

In April 2026 the D-I Cabinet adopted men's wrestling Proposal 2026-12 as final, effective immediately, for prospects initially enrolling on or after **Aug. 1, 2028**. The Cabinet report does not print the new dates. - **House settlement:** no T1 change to wrestling roster limits found in the window.

### Cited Findings
- **Men's wrestling recruiting dates: adopted (T1, April 2026; older than the window, takes effect for class of 2028+).** Proposal No. 2026-12, "Recruiting – Men's Wrestling – First Date for Correspondence, Telephone Calls, Evaluations and Contacts and Campus Visits." The Cabinet "adopted Proposal No. 2026-12, as expedited legislation… effective immediately, applicable to prospective student-athletes who will initially enroll full time at a collegiate institution on or after August 1, 2028." Legislative action table: "Adopted (Final)." — [Report of the NCAA Division I Cabinet, April 14-15, 2026, NCAA](https://ncaaorg.s3.amazonaws.com/committees/d1/cabinet/Apr2026D1Cabinet_Apr14Report.pdf), T1, read 2026-10-08
- **The concept behind it (T1, Feb 2026).** The D-I Men's Wrestling Oversight Committee (Feb. 3, 2026) supported a "tiered" concept:

  - "June 15 after prospective student-athlete's junior year of high school: Evaluations and contacts"
  - "August 15 of prospective student-athlete's senior year of high school: Official and unofficial visits"

  Rationale: it "accounts for a prospective student-athlete's continued physical growth and development". Source: [Report of the NCAA Division I Men's Wrestling Oversight Committee, Feb. 3, 2026](https://ncaaorg.s3.amazonaws.com/championships/sports/wrestling/d1/men/Feb2026D1MWR_CommitteeReport.pdf), T1, read 2026-10-08

Women's wrestling is not mentioned, and no wrestling-specific effective date is stated. — [Division I Cabinet adopts new transfer windows in several sports, NCAA.org, 2026-01-14](https://www.ncaa.org/media-center-division-i-cabinet-adopts-new-transfer-windows-in-several-sports/), T1, read 2026-10-08
- **Foreign tours (T1, April 2026).** The Cabinet amended Proposal 2026-8 to apply to all sports, permitting a foreign tour no more than once per year, effective immediately. The men's wrestling oversight committee (Feb 2026) and the women's committee (Mar 2026) both supported applying it to wrestling. — [D-I Cabinet report, Apr 14-15, 2026](https://ncaaorg.s3.amazonaws.com/committees/d1/cabinet/Apr2026D1Cabinet_Apr14Report.pdf), T1

I saw this in an NCAA release reprinted on member and NGB sites, but did not open the NCAA original, so it is not used as a claim. Fact log has no row on this; no change found for 2027.

### Cited Findings
