Suggested fact-log rows from the rules book (Articles: `lacrosse/mens/guide/coaches-look-for-by-position`):
- Men's: **10 players**; designated goalkeeper, defense, midfield, attack (no fixed count per position); **field 110 × 60 yards**; **maximum four long crosses (52–72 in.) besides the goalkeeper's**; short crosse 40–42 in.; goalkeeper crosse 10–12 in. wide, field-crosse head 6–10 in.; **four 15-minute periods**; faceoff at the start of each period and after each goal (absent penalty/extra-man situations); offside = more than 6 in the offensive half or more than 7 in the defensive half; live-ball substitution. — 2027 and 2028 NCAA Men's Lacrosse Rules (Aug. 2026), Rules 1-1, 1-2, 1-17, 2-1, 3-1, 4-3, 4-12, 4-23. **This closes the men's half of §G row "PENDING (partly closed 2026-10-08) — remaining NCAA playing rules as stated,"** except the "3 attack, 3 midfield, 3 defense, 1 goalie" breakdown, which the rules book does not contain and which was removed from copy.
- Faceoffs, saves, clears and caused turnovers are official NCAA statistics; faceoff win-loss %, save % and clearing % are calculated categories. — NCAA Men's Lacrosse Statisticians' Manual; rules book Appendix V.
