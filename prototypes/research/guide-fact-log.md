# RosterWise — Sport-Agnostic Guide Fact Log

**Purpose:** one row per published claim on the sport-agnostic pages under
`src/guide/` → primary source → date verified → article(s) that use it.
Lives in `prototypes/` so it never deploys (build only emits from `src/`).
Before flipping any article to live, check every number/date/rule in its copy
against a row here.

**Verification date for this pass:** 2026-08-28

**2026-10-09 official-visit pass:** the three §C rows dated 2026-10-09 were read
on that date in the NCAA Legislative Services Database (web3.ncaa.org/lsdbi) —
the current Division I Bylaw 13.6 text, and Proposals 2022-32 and 2026-4 with
their strikethrough markup preserved (read in a rendering browser, wrapping
struck and underlined runs so the deletions were visible). Nothing else on the
guide pages was re-verified on that date, so neither page's `date:` was bumped.

**Why this log exists:** it was created during the remediation of the
independent audit of 2026-08-28. Until then the `src/guide/` pages — the
highest-traffic, most cross-linked content on the site — had **no fact log at
all**, which is why a superseded NCAA test-score regime, an NCAA-wide framing of
a Division I-only NLI change, and a Division III visit date attached to Division I
women's lacrosse all survived on them while the sport pages that carried the same
claims were corrected. Three of the audit's findings were blast-radius failures
of exactly this kind. This log is the index that should have existed.

**Sourcing rules honored:** Tier 1 only for every rule, date and count below —
NCAA.org's own initial-eligibility pages, NCAA media-center releases, the NCAA's
House implementation Q&A and the Division II Coaches Off-Campus Recruiting Guide
on `ncaaorg.s3.amazonaws.com`, and the Division I/III Manuals via LSDBi. No
aggregators, no recruiting-service blogs. Where a law firm's reading of NCAA data
had been supporting a count (`nil-and-revenue-sharing`, the international share),
the count was replaced with RosterWise's own first-party figure — see §E.

**Two source-reading disciplines this pass enforced,** both of which the audit
itself tripped over:

1. **Multi-column PDFs must be read visually, never from text extraction.** The
   NCAA recruiting calendars are column grids; a linear text dump interleaves
   the columns and silently reassigns entries to the wrong sport. Two audit
   findings (items 6 and 9c) were produced this way and are recorded below as
   **NOT ERRORS**.
2. **A "keep this wording" instruction in a fact log is itself perishable** and
   must be re-checked against the source on every pass, exactly like a number.
