# RosterWise — Governance Counts & Revenue-Share Allocation Fact Log

**Purpose:** one row per claim researched, sourced, re-stated, or cut while
remediating findings **H2** (program and membership counts stated as unsourced
approximations) and **H3** (the revenue-share allocation split presented as
structure) from `reports/content-audit-2026-08-25.md`.
Lives in `prototypes/` so it never deploys (build only emits from `src/`).

**Verification date for this pass:** 2026-08-26

**Sourcing rules honored:** Tier 1 only. Every count below was read first-hand
from the governing body that publishes it — NCAA.org's own membership and
sport-sponsorship tables and the NCAA research PDF on
`ncaaorg.s3.amazonaws.com`, NAIA.org's own membership pages, and the NCAA's
House implementation Q&A on `ncaaorg.s3.amazonaws.com`. No aggregators, no
recruiting-service blogs, no news outlets supporting any count or rule. Where a
governing body does **not** publish a count (NAIA and NJCAA men's lacrosse
sponsorship), the number was **cut** and the copy phrased structurally — see
section E.

**Membership ≠ sponsorship.** These are two different numbers and the old copy
blurred them. "NCAA Division III member institutions" (§A) is how many schools
belong to the division. "NCAA Division III programs sponsoring men's lacrosse"
(§C) is how many of them field that one team. They are logged separately below
and are separately labeled in the copy.

## D. House settlement — the benefits cap is institution-wide and allocation is discretionary (H3)

Primary source for this entire section:
**NCAA, "Question and Answer: Implementation of the House Settlement," updated
February 11, 2026**, Section D (Institutional Benefits and the Benefits Cap) —
`https://ncaaorg.s3.amazonaws.com/governance/d1/legislation/Feb2026D1Gov_PhaseSevenSetQuestionandAnswer.pdf`.
The full 2,098-line document was read for this pass, not just Section D.

| Claim | Primary source | Verified | Articles |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The benefits cap is **"the maximum dollar value of revenue share payments or benefits a Participating Institution may provide to its student-athletes during an academic year (i.e., July 1 through June 30)."** Quoted verbatim in copy. | NCAA House Q&A (2026-02-11), **Question No. D2** | 2026-08-26 | /guide/nil-and-revenue-sharing/, /lacrosse/guide/house-settlement-and-lacrosse/ |
| **"Each Participating Institution may provide benefits, at its discretion, to a student-athlete as long as the combined value of the new benefits … provided by or on behalf of the Participating Institution to all student-athletes at the Participating Institution does not exceed the benefits cap at any time during the academic year."** The cap binds the *institution's total*, not any sport's share. Quoted verbatim in copy. | NCAA House Q&A (2026-02-11), **Question No. D2**, paragraph 2 | 2026-08-26 | /guide/nil-and-revenue-sharing/, /lacrosse/guide/house-settlement-and-lacrosse/ |
| **The Q&A sets no allocation of the benefits pool among sports.** Verified by reading the complete document and searching it for every allocation-, distribution-, discretion-, sport- and Title IX-related term; Section D governs the size and administration of the cap only. The only allocation language in Section D is the "at its discretion" clause above. | NCAA House Q&A (2026-02-11), full document, Section D | 2026-08-26 | /guide/nil-and-revenue-sharing/, /lacrosse/guide/house-settlement-and-lacrosse/ |
| **The 2025-26 benefits cap is $20.5 million.** Carried forward unchanged from the M1 pass; re-read in the same document on this verification date. | NCAA House Q&A (2026-02-11), **Question No. D3** | 2026-08-26 | /guide/nil-and-revenue-sharing/, /lacrosse/guide/house-settlement-and-lacrosse/ — and see `audit-m1-m2-fact-log.md` §A for the full list of pages carrying this figure |

**Replaces:** the 75 / 15 / 5 / 5 allocation split at
`/guide/nil-and-revenue-sharing/` and "typically around 75%" at
`/lacrosse/guide/house-settlement-and-lacrosse/`. See section E for what was cut

## F. Open items to re-check before/at publish

- **NCAA membership counts (§A) are the 2025-26 breakdown, as of September 2025.** The NCAA publishes a new breakdown each fall. Re-verify at the 2026-27 rollover; four pages carry these figures.
- **NCAA sport-sponsorship counts (§C) are NCAA *projections* for 2025-26**, carrying the NCAA's own "actual numbers may change" caveat. The NCAA's Sport Sponsorship and Participation Rates Report publishes actuals on a lag — re-verify against actuals when they appear, and against the 2026-27 projections each fall.
- **The NAIA's 250 figure (§B) carries no academic-year anchor.** It is attributed to NAIA.org rather than to a season. Re-read it each season; if the NAIA ever publishes a season-anchored count, switch to that.
- **NAIA and NJCAA men's lacrosse counts remain unsourceable (§E).** If either body begins publishing a sponsorship count, the two lacrosse pages can be filled in — until then they stay structural.
- **The 2026-27 benefits cap is still unpublished** (carried over from the M1 pass). `/guide/nil-and-revenue-sharing/` and `/lacrosse/guide/house-settlement-and-lacrosse/` state the 2025-26 figure and say so.
