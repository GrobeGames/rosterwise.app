## guide/age-based-eligibility.md  (stays 2026-10-08)

> **Correction, October 8, 2026.** The August 3 version of this page had three errors and has since been overtaken by a major development. (1) It dated Judge Sweeney's clarification order August 3; the order is dated **August 2, 2026**. (2) It described the class of athletes covered by the injunction as the "high school class of 2022." The court defines the class by when athletes **began college competition (the 2022-23 season)** and whether they completed four seasons under the old rules by the end of 2025-26. (3) It said those athletes could play a fifth season "rather than waiting for the age-based model to phase in." They were not waiting for anything: under the NCAA's transition, athletes who used their final season in 2025-26 get **no additional eligibility**. (4) The material change: on **August 21, 2026 the Tenth Circuit stayed the injunction**, so the fifth season it allowed is **not currently in effect**. We also removed three statements about the clarification order that came only from news reporting and do not appear in the order itself, and an NCAA quote we could no longer source.
