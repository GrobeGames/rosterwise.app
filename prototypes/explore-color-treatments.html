RosterWise · Volleyball

Explore Tab — Color & Formatting Treatments

Same layout as today (stat row → Search Programs hero → Divisions/Conferences → Connections/Program Intel → tab bar). Only the palette, typography, depth, and card formatting change. The court line-art stays in the hero across all five; no emoji anywhere. Each treatment is a complete, swappable theme.

1

Midnight & Gold

Premium dark

Deep navy with a restrained gold accent and hairline gold card borders. The most “premium product” read — reframes the current dark cards as luxe rather than flat. Gold is used sparingly (eyebrows, accents, numerals) so it never tips into gaudy.

2

Paper

Clean light

Flips to a light background with white bordered cards, soft shadows, and the brand blue reserved for the hero and accents. Bright, trustworthy, and the most App-Store-screenshot-friendly. Best legibility in daylight use at tournaments.

3

Duotone Blue

Tonal / cohesive

A single blue family from deep navy to sky, with a light-blue accent. Highly cohesive and calm — no competing colors. Keeps the current blue brand but makes it feel intentional and modern through tonal layering rather than gradients-on-gradients.

4

Blackout

High contrast

Near-black canvas, one electric gold accent, monospaced numerals, and a bold gold hero block. Sporty and energetic with maximum contrast — punchy on OLED. The tighter 14px radius and mono digits give it a confident, athletic edge.

5

Warm Editorial

Cream + serif

Cream canvas, ink-brown text, a terracotta accent, and Playfair serif for titles and numerals. Sophisticated and distinctly un-sporty — positions RosterWise as a considered, editorial guide rather than a stats app. Warm and approachable for parents.

Quick take: 1 (Midnight & Gold) is the most premium evolution of today’s dark look. 2 (Paper) is the boldest, most differentiated move and best for App Store screenshots. 3 (Duotone Blue) is the safest refinement of the current brand. 4 (Blackout) is the most athletic/energetic. 5 (Warm Editorial) is the most distinctive repositioning.
